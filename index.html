HerDigital Horizon

Discover. Learn. Connect. Grow.

A proposed digital career-discovery initiative for girls and young women exploring information and communication technology, cybersecurity and technology-enabled industries — before deciding what to study or become.

Explore digital fields Read the concept

Development status: concept development. Nothing below has launched yet — read what that means.

I finished secondary school without a clear picture of what technology could offer me. I want the next girl to have one.

That gap became obvious again during the 2026 Girls in ICT mentorship speed-dating session, where the same questions kept coming up: what fields actually exist, what do people in them do day to day, and where does someone with no background even start?

Cybersecurity is the field I know best, through my work at the Public Utilities Regulatory Authority and my own path into ICT. But the real problem is broader than one field — it's that very few girls get a map of the digital world before they're asked to pick a direction on it.

HerDigital Horizon is a response to that gap. It starts small, as a concept, with one honest aim: help a girl see what's out there before she has to choose.

Explore digital fields

Technology shows up inside almost every industry — health, fashion, agriculture, finance, energy. These are illustrative starting points, not a final programme structure.

Cybersecurity

Protecting systems, data and people online. Covers digital safety, incident response and security operations.

Software & development

Building the programs, apps and websites people use every day. Covers programming and web development.

Data & AI

Finding patterns in information and building systems that learn from it. Covers data analysis and artificial intelligence.

Digital design

Shaping how digital products look, feel and work for the people using them. Covers UI/UX and digital media.

Digital health

Applying technology to healthcare delivery and services. Covers health informatics and digital health systems.

Technology in business

Using digital tools to run and grow organisations. Covers fintech, digital business and project management.

A full page per field — what people in it actually do, the skills that help, and how it connects to other industries — is planned but not yet written.

Next step

Planning the first workshop

After this website, the first concrete piece of HerDigital Horizon is a reusable introductory workshop: a presentation, an interactive activity, clear learning objectives and a way to collect feedback from whoever attends.

No workshop has been delivered under this initiative yet. I plan to develop and pilot it after completing my master's studies, so it can draw on what I learn there.

Built on real experience

This isn't a hobby idea with no background behind it. Two programmes shaped it directly.

2026

Girls in ICT — Organising Committee

Helped run the mentorship speed-dating session where this idea first took shape — the same handful of questions came up again and again from girls who simply didn't know what the field offered.

Sep 2026

Her CyberTracks — Incident Response Track, ITU

A five-month training and mentoring programme for women in cybersecurity, with an on-site regional training in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Comparing notes with women on other tracks about how they found their way in is part of what convinced me this needs to exist for girls much earlier.

Photos from these programmes aren't posted here yet — only once it's confirmed that sharing them is okay with everyone pictured.

What's ahead

These are the ideas the initiative is built around. None of them are live. Listing them here is about being clear on direction, not announcing a launch.

Learn: foundations

A set of beginner-friendly starting points — digital literacy, how the internet works, an introduction to programming, digital safety, an introduction to data and AI. Curated from existing free resources rather than built from scratch.

Find your path: a quiz

A short, interest-based quiz suggesting a few fields worth exploring — not a career test or professional assessment. It won't go live until the results and the reasoning behind them are written clearly enough to not be mistaken for advice.

Connect: mentors

Women working in these fields sharing their path and answering questions. Because this would involve young women asking questions of adults they don't know, it won't open until there's a real consent, safeguarding and moderation process behind it — not just a sign-up form.

Opportunities

A running list of workshops, free courses, scholarships and events worth knowing about — starting with whatever HerDigital Horizon runs itself.

The concept, in short

Vision
A future where girls and young women have the knowledge, confidence and access to opportunities to participate meaningfully in the digital world.
Mission
Make digital education and career guidance more accessible by connecting girls and young women with learning resources, career pathways, role models and supportive communities.
Status
Concept development. The site and resources are being planned as the first stage of the initiative. Community activity, mentorship and partnerships will be explored as it develops — and described here honestly as they happen, not before.
